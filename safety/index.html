Safety Guidelines

Your safety matters to us. These guidelines are designed to help you have safer and more secure interactions while using UnicalDGM.

1. General Safety

  • UnicalDGM is a listing and advertising platform only. We do not inspect or verify goods and services physically.
  • Always use good judgment when interacting with buyers or vendors.
  • If an offer feels suspicious or too good to be true, take a step back and reassess.

2. Meeting Buyers or Vendors

  • Meet in public, busy, and well-lit locations whenever possible.
  • Avoid isolated or unfamiliar areas.
  • Let a friend or trusted person know before meeting someone.
  • If you feel unsure, go along with someone for added safety.

3. Payments & Transactions

  • Avoid making full payments before confirming the product or service.
  • Be cautious about sending advance payments, especially to unfamiliar vendors.
  • Keep records of conversations, receipts, and transaction details.
  • UnicalDGM does not guarantee refunds for transactions made between users.

4. Protecting Your Information

  • Do not share sensitive personal information such as your home address, ID details, or banking information unnecessarily.
  • Keep communication clear, respectful, and professional.

5. Vendor Verification

  • Some vendors may have a verification badge based on submitted details.
  • Verification does not guarantee quality, reliability, or delivery.
  • Always carry out your own checks before making a decision.

6. Buying Safely

  • Inspect items properly before making payment.
  • Ask questions about product condition, durability, or return options.
  • Avoid rushed decisions or pressure from sellers.

7. Selling Safely

  • Meet buyers in safe and public locations.
  • Confirm payment before handing over goods.
  • Be alert to fake payment alerts or edited receipts.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive financial or personal details.

8. Prohibited & Risky Activities

Do not engage in:

  • Illegal transactions
  • Fraudulent or deceptive activities
  • Buying or selling stolen items
  • Any activity that violates local laws or institutional rules

Such actions may lead to removal from the Platform and possible reporting.

9. Reporting Concerns

If you notice suspicious, unsafe, or fraudulent behaviour:

  • End communication immediately
  • Do not proceed with the transaction
  • Report the user or listing through UnicalDGM’s official contact channels

10. Disclaimer

UnicalDGM is not responsible for any loss, damage, injury, or disputes resulting from interactions between users. All transactions are carried out at your own risk.

11. Stay Alert

Your safety comes first. Trust your instincts and never prioritise completing a transaction over your personal well-being.

By using Unical Digital Marketing (UnicalDGM), you acknowledge that you have read and understood these Safety Guidelines.
